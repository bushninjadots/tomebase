# Team Setup

Invite teammates, assign roles, and collaborate on documentation.

## How Teams Work

A personal team is auto-created when you sign up. All projects belong to a team. Team members can view and edit all projects within the team.

## Inviting Members

1. Go to **Team Settings** from the dashboard
2. Click **Invite Member**
3. Enter the email address
4. Choose a role: **Admin** or **Member**
5. Send the invite

Invite links expire after 7 days. Unaccepted invites can be revoked.

## Roles

| Role | Permissions |
|------|-------------|
| Admin | Full access — manage projects, settings, members, and billing |
| Member | Read and write pages, view project settings |

## Member Limits

| Tier | Max Members |
|------|-------------|
| Free | 5 |
| Pro | 15 |
| Enterprise | 100 |
